# Related hardware: Xperia X10 Mini E10i teardown

Source: [iFixit — Sony Ericsson Xperia X10 Mini E10i Teardown](https://www.ifixit.com/Teardown/Sony+Ericsson+Xperia+X10+Mini+E10i+Teardown/3124), accessed 2026-10-25 through the [iFixit guide API](https://www.ifixit.com/api/2.0/guides/3124).

## Scope

**PUBLISHED, related model:** This teardown covers the **E10i X10 Mini**, not our **U20a X10 mini pro** with its sliding keyboard. It provides comparison material, not verified U20a specifications or disassembly instructions.

## Useful observations from the teardown

- Step 1 reports a Qualcomm MSM7227 at 600 MHz, 256 MB RAM and 256 MB ROM, with 128 MB accessible. These memory figures remain unverified for our handset.
- Steps 2 and 5 show micro-USB connectivity and removable microSD storage.
- Steps 6–10 illustrate T5 Torx fasteners, antenna pressure contacts, battery and display connections, and motherboard removal.
- Step 8 describes an E10i battery rated 3.7 V / 950 mAh. Do not substitute this for the U20a battery specification.

## Use in this lab

Use the photographs to learn component recognition and compare board construction. Do not assume connector locations, battery design, parts compatibility, or opening sequence transfer to the mini pro. Record our device's memory through ADB before promoting related-model figures into its hardware profile.
